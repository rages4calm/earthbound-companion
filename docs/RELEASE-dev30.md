# Redux dev.30 - Credits photos and widescreen PSI effects

Credits photographs could display bright diagonal stripes and scrambled frame graphics. The shared map loader copied a normal 0x7000-byte tileset into VRAM during photograph loading, overwriting the credits frame. Photo loading now follows the original 0x4000-byte transfer limit, reloads the photo tileset even when its cached identifier matches, and skips normal map overlay/animation loading. Existing credits checkpoints restore the frame graphics after PPU unpack, without changing the save format or advancing the ending.

During battles with large artwork such as Giygas and Porky, PSI animations could repeat into the left and right widescreen gutters. The shared PSI layer inherited the battle background's repeating viewport mode. Every active PSI animation now uses the centered native screen slice; the battle background keeps its wide presentation. This applies across PSI animations, not just Freeze, and is derived again when an existing checkpoint resumes.

Verification: the reported credits checkpoint reproduced the stripes with dev.29 and rendered cleanly with dev.30. Its private copy completed the remaining credits naturally. Prepared ending transactions captured all 32 photo scenes in Original and Redux and checked the frame graphics at each capture. Prepared PSI projection checks covered 34 Original and 68 Redux animation definitions, both layer bit depths and three horizontal targets: 612 cases and 9,774 sampled frames. A diagnostic white palette isolates projection geometry; these checks do not certify effect colors or every frame. The old binary reproduces gutter pixels and the new binary confines the samples to the native slice, then restores the background viewport mode when each effect completes. Two earlier private test dialogs came from an incorrect capture-count assertion; the corrected harness logs failures without desktop assertion popups.

The user reports completing a Redux campaign and remembered side activities, including Giygas and credits. Equipment and stats were boosted for final testing. This is useful playtest evidence, not exhaustive compatibility certification. All prior fixes, packs, personal saves, settings and music are retained. No personal recovery or save edits are included in this update, and no ROM, extracted packs, soundtrack or saves are distributed. Fresh automated full campaigns, randomized campaigns, all combat combinations, pixel-perfect comparisons, physical-controller input and audible music remain outside this verification.
