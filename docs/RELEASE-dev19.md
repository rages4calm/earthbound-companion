# Release checkpoint — dev.19

Dev.19 ships two already verified changes: a Redux conversion correction for accented party-target names, and intentional Companion QoL corrections for equipped-item Give transactions. It retains all dev.18 presentation, movement, save, audio and randomizer fixes. No content pack, save format, seed identity or progression policy changes in this release.

The [shipping provenance](../research/native-runtime-dev19-provenance.json) binds the exact complete shipping player and paired observer to the [frozen combined proposal](../research/combined-menu-gear-dev19-v1-final-manifest.json). The release uses the player binary already exercised by those tests; native replacement objects were not used.

- 128 selected Goods/PSI and service party-selection parent sequences, including 64 fresh-process continuations, plus 360 bounded rendering observations passed.
- 732 selected Goods/Give/Drop/Equip/Use parent executions across Original/Redux and player/observer passed; these use 366 frozen distinct fixtures per build, with 116 cold continuations and warm/cold comparisons.
- Generic ASCII labels, tiny titles, separate HPPP name rendering, serialized structures and original/ASCII names retain checked controls.
- The [completed special-mode parent tests](../research/special-mode-parent-dev28-dev18-v2-manifest.json) retain their dev.18-v2 runtime identity: 60 prepared runs, 24 cold restores, selected Magicant room warps and robot landing/walking. They are not relabeled as dev.19 tests or proof of natural quest reachability.

Prepared prerequisites are explicit in the reports. They exercise actual native parents and effects afterward, but do not prove the quests that normally reach them, every item/stat boundary, controller hardware or full delivered audio. Earlier equipment/sprite/terrain and cinematic checks retain their original release identities. The bounded multi-turn battle pilot stopped with five encounters incomplete at its harness limit; that is diagnostic work, not a confirmed game defect or a passing full battle audit.

## During your playthrough

- Continue using your current saves. Content identity and save format are unchanged from dev.18.
- A previously saved open menu whose name already contains `?` must be closed and reopened once to repopulate its label.
- Report location, game mode/seed, action, and an F6 state when possible. We now fix and verify reported issues as playtesting continues.

The autonomous audit and agents are stopped. Native implementations exist for the reviewed normal-play Redux features; no further whole feature is currently confirmed missing. Complete story/randomized playthroughs, natural photo acquisition, all combat/music transitions, wet-terrain/device/display coverage and exhaustive compatibility remain unverified. This is an experimental native adaptation, not a claim of 100% parity.
