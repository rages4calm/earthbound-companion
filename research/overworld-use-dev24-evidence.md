# Complete selected Goods and PSI use parents

On the frozen dev17-v4 native library, all 155 Original and all 155 Redux selected pause-menu parents pass. These 310 executions include 24 fresh-process child continuations. Eighteen explicit warm/cold comparisons match final HP/PP targets, all seven status groups and complete inventory, key pool, storage and queued-delivery state. No production source changes or native defects were established by this pass.

The private driver starts at the actual pause-menu entry with initial levels, party, inventory and afflictions prepared beforehand. Menu choices become real platform D-pad/A/B pulses. The production Goods/PSI menu, target selection, packed description text, battle action, effect children, writeback and pause-menu cleanup run normally. No handler, menu result, post-entry status/inventory change or completion signal is supplied by the fixture.

Selected cases cover Wet towel, Refreshing herb, Secret herb and Horn of life; Healing alpha/beta/gamma/omega; Lifeup alpha/beta/gamma/omega; and wrong-user or battle-only Bazooka refusal. They include source treatment priority, selected PCs, single-user auto-targeting, multiple PSI users with Poo selected, four-party healing, an unconscious member during all-party healing, exact item consumption/PP charges, no-effect use, failed revival, insufficient PP, target cancellation and backout, and holder/caster restrictions. Actual windows are closed when the full parent returns.

Gamma revival uses the original `SUCCESS_255` rule: the real next serialized RNG byte must be less than 192, then HP becomes one quarter of maximum and all status groups clear. A read-only peek restores RNG state before the actual callback consumes its draw. Each profile includes 24 item and 24 PSI revival cases. Original produced 18 item successes/6 failures and 17 PSI successes/7 failures; Redux produced 19 item successes/5 failures and 17 PSI successes/7 failures. The different selected draw positions are recorded; this is not a claim that complete Original and Redux text timelines consume identical RNG.

Cold checkpoints capture the production targeting entry, actual effect action entry, revival child, PP-refusal continuation, and the third all-party action after two party members were healed. Restoring in a fresh process does not skip or repeat those members, charge PP again, or consume another item. The observer executable is identified but was not separately executed. Sound traces prove requests only. Current rolling-meter/frame counters and graphics/audio delivery are outside the semantic comparisons.

The separate source report passes 69 checks: selected Original ROM table rows, pinned Redux numeric table rows and ordered Original disassembly control-flow tokens. Source verification publishes addresses, numeric metadata and hashes only. It is not original SNES CPU execution. Concurrent affliction fixtures exercise writeback and priority; their preparation does not establish that every combination is naturally reachable outside combat.

Three diagnostic assumptions were corrected before the strict final runs. Higher Healing tiers cure strangeness before falling back to alpha; initial concurrent strange/cold fixtures therefore had the wrong expected priority. The original Goods `CLC; SBC` guard suppresses Use for unconscious/diamondized holders while allowing a paralyzed holder. The all-party loop's internal iteration stage does not yield, so the mid-party capture now observes its real third action child instead. Earlier pilot reports are fixture diagnostics, not native-defect evidence, and are not the final verification corpus.
